Library

Sources is what the planner uses: ticking a book on or off costs nothing and deletes nothing. Manage is what is stored in this browser — importing, refreshing and removing content live there.

Load a 5etools data export: drop the .zip, drop or choose the unzipped folder (it is remembered, and powers Refresh), or stage individual JSON files. Everything is parsed and stored in this browser — nothing is uploaded.

Homebrew & Unearthed Arcana work too. Download any brew as JSON from the 5etools homebrew repository (D&D Beyond drops live there), or UA from the prerelease repository — around 60 KB each. A cloned repository also scans fine as a folder: it is filed by category (spell/, class/, collection/…), so one brew's content scatters across folders, and the book list below is how you find it. Not the repository's “Download ZIP” — that is every brew plus artwork, gigabytes no browser tab can open.

This planner ships without spell data. Load a 5etools data export to fill it with spells, classes, subclasses, feats and species. Everything is parsed and stored in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Your books

A tick means this book is in my data. Unticking one you already have removes its content from this browser — the only way to get the space back. A dimmed row is a book the scanned folder offers that you haven't imported: tick it and Apply to pull it in. Nothing is written until you press Apply, and Apply says how many books it would remove before you do.

Your builds are never touched. A pick whose book is gone is flagged in the banner at the top of the app, not deleted — re-import that book and it resolves again.
