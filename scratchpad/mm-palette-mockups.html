PAL · three complete palettes, with the numbers

The complaint, measured: the current accent sits 3–6° of hue from the alert red at near-identical lightness — the two are the same colour to fast reading. Each proposal below is a FULL palette (both themes shown); functional colours side by side so the separation is judged where it failed. Contrast figures are WCAG ratios on the theme background (4.5:1 = AA normal text).
